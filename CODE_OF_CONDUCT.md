# Code of Conduct

We want LifeLedger to be a welcoming, respectful community. Be considerate,
give constructive feedback, respect differing experiences, and protect other
people's privacy. Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual
attention, and publishing private information without consent are unacceptable.

This applies in project spaces and when representing the project. Maintainers
may remove content or restrict participation to protect the community.

To report a concern, contact a repository maintainer privately through GitHub.
Provide only the information needed to explain the situation. Maintainers will
handle reports as discreetly as practical, review them fairly, and take
appropriate action. Do not post sensitive details publicly.
